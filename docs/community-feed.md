# Community feed and guided posting

The public community has separate For You/Following views and opt-in topic/institute filters. The first 20 records stay live, older pages load on demand. New arrivals are buffered behind an explicit button so reading position is preserved. Pull refresh reloads the source. Popular uses the existing aggregate engagement counter. Filter/search counts describe loaded pages; Load more remains available when a filtered page has no matches. Private profiles are excluded from the People search.

Each user selects one of Like, Insightful, Helpful, Celebrate or Support. Post.reactions maps immutable UID to reaction; legacy likedBy members without a mapped choice remain Like. likesCount/likedBy continue to represent unique reactors for existing ranking and compatibility. Transactions change only the current UID; rules validate membership and exact totals. Comments and votes preserve reaction/template metadata in Demo as well as real mode. Votes require exact count deltas, one own ballot, and an open deadline. Voted poll options and deadlines are immutable. New comments/counts and deleting a selected best answer are atomic.

Ready forms cover questions, notes/resources, admissions, scholarships, study partners/groups, exam/assignment discussions, achievements, polls, career/internships, and general posts. Structured public fields are in educationalData; legacy text-only posts still render. Public audience is explicit. Profile course/semester suggestions can be edited. Announcement posts carry Community shared; green account verification does not certify announcements. Public posts do not offer private group audience options.

Drafts are local, keyed by real/Demo identity and post ID; restored drafts never cross accounts. Demo photos are local and must be reattached after restoring a draft. Production attachment URLs persist in drafts. Uploads show progress and preserve text on failure; files are limited to 10 MB and 8 per post. New Storage objects use community/<owner UID>/<upload ID>/<filename>. Public-community media is readable by signed-in members; no private group media support is implied. Native sharing sends readable post text/source links rather than inventing an unconfigured deep-link domain.

Hidden/not-interested posts and muted authors are owner-only preferences. Feed preferences can restore hidden/muted/blocked entries. Block removes follow edges; unblocking does not re-follow. Demo stays in memory and never uploads, reports or modifies production data. Permissions and widget tests cover reactions, posting, drafts, buffered updates, storage ownership and narrow/dark layouts.

Current search is local over loaded pages. Following/topic filters do not claim a complete server-side personalized recommendation index; progressively loading more pages finds older matches. Long-term high-volume feeds can migrate to a server-side index without changing the public post schema.
